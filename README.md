### 📂 Featured Apps & Open Source ([goork.de](https://goork.de))

#### ⭐ [SongFlip](https://github.com/thefraggle/songflip)
> *“Sharing music with friends should be effortless. SongFlip removes platform friction so everyone can enjoy songs in their favorite player without extra clicks.”*

Automatic, zero-click music & podcast link redirector for Android & iOS across **14+ services** (Spotify, Apple Music & Podcasts, YouTube Music, Tidal, Qobuz, Deezer, Amazon Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Beatport, Audiomack, Idagio, Anghami, Pocket Casts).
- **Highlights:** 0-click background redirect, *Listen & Flip* music recognition (incl. Offline Club Mode), Universal Playlist Converter & Web Sharing (`songflip.link/p/...`), Audiophile Quality Matcher.
- **Press:** 📰 **[Featured on Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/songflip-music-link-redirect-android-3711432/)**
- *License:* GPL-3.0 | *Stack:* Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP), Jetpack Compose, Swift, SwiftUI, Ktor, Firebase Cloud Functions, RevenueCat, Aptabase  
👉 **[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.goork.songflip)** | **[iOS Beta (TestFlight)](https://testflight.apple.com/join/c2ceF8ZK)** | **[songflip.link](https://songflip.link)** | **[Source Code](https://github.com/thefraggle/songflip)**
